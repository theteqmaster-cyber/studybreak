9.2 Master Formula Sheet — Part 1

Formulas: CBA, FPA & COCOMO

COST-BENEFIT ANALYSISCONCEPT 2
  • Net Profit = Total Benefits − Total Costs
  • Payback = Year before recovery + (Unrecovered / Recovery Yr Inflow)
  • ROI = (Avg Annual Profit / Total Investment) × 100%
  • NPV = Σ [NCF_t / (1 + r)^t] − Cost_0 (Yr 0 DF = 1.0000)
  • IRR = discount rate r where NPV = 0
ESTIMATION & RISKCONCEPTS 4 & 7
  • FP = UAF × VAF (Albrecht: 5 components × weights; 14 GSCs 0–5)
  • Rule of Thumb: 1 FP ≈ 2 days effort
  • Basic COCOMO: Effort = c × (KDSI)^k (Person-Months)
  • Risk Exposure (RE) = Probability × Impact
  • Defect Density = Defects / 1 KLOC
9.3 Master Formula Sheet — Part 2

Formulas: PERT / CPM & EVM

PERT & CPM SCHEDULINGCONCEPT 5
  • t_e = (a + 4m + b) / 6 (Expected PERT Duration)
  • σ = (b − a) / 6 | σ² = [(b − a) / 6]²
  • σ_path = √(Σ σ_i²) (Sum variances along critical path)
  • Float = LS − ES = LF − EF (Critical path Float = 0)
EARNED VALUE MANAGEMENTCONCEPT 6
  • SV = EV − PV | CV = EV − AC
  • SPI = EV / PV | CPI = EV / AC
  • EAC (Typical) = BAC / CPI | (Atypical) = AC + (BAC − EV)
  • EAC (CPI×SPI) = AC + (BAC − EV)/(CPI × SPI)
  • ETC = EAC − AC | VAC = BAC − EAC
  • Est. Duration = Planned Duration / SPI
  • TCPI = (BAC − EV) / (BAC − AC)
9.4 Avoid Easy Zeroes

Top 8 Exam Traps Checklist

#Common MistakeCorrect Rule for Full Marks
1Calling Software Flexibility an advantageFlexibility invites scope creep — it is a management challenge!
2Using routine payroll as a project exampleRoutine operations ≠ project; building a new system by a deadline is a project.
3Discounting Year 0 cash outlay in NPVYear 0 happens today: discount factor is always 1.0000.
4Choosing a project by Net Profit over NPVAlways rank mutually exclusive projects using NPV (Time Value of Money).
5Writing only numbers in EVM answersAlways explain what SV/CV/SPI/CPI/TCPI mean to the PM!
6Guessing which EAC formula to useIf variance type isn't stated, default to Typical: BAC / CPI.
7Putting < 3 causes per Ishikawa branchInclude all 6Ms with at least 3 causes per bone.
8Mixing up Quality vs. Grade & QA vs. QCQuality = fit for use; QA = proactive defect prevention; QC = reactive testing.
