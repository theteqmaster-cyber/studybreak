🔬 SCS2110: Research Methods

Pilot Card — Research Formulations, Qualitative vs Quantitative & Hypothesis Testing

1. Research Problem Formulation

Defining clear research questions ($RQ_1, RQ_2$), identifying knowledge gaps, and establishing testable null ($H_0$) and alternative ($H_1$) hypotheses.

2. Qualitative vs Quantitative Paradigms

Quantitative: Numerical measurement, statistical validation, surveys, controlled experiments, ANOVA, t-tests.

Qualitative: Grounded theory, thematic analysis, semi-structured interviews, case studies.

3. Sampling Strategies & Ethics

Probability Sampling (Random, Stratified, Cluster) vs Non-Probability Sampling (Purposive, Snowball). Ethical consent, data anonymization, and reproducibility.
