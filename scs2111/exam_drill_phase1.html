Exam Drill Phase 1 of 4 70 Individual Questions (Strict Difficulty Order)

Phase 1 Master Exam Bank: Foundations, Signals & Channel Capacity

Complete 70-question university question bank structured strictly by difficulty: Questions 1–30 Easy, Questions 31–40 Medium, and Questions 41–70 Hard.

Part 1: Easy / Foundational Questions (Questions 01 – 30)

Question 01 • Easy [4 Marks]

What is Data Communications?

Official Model Answer & Marking Scheme [4 Marks]:

Data Communications is the exchange of data between two devices via a transmission medium governed by protocol rules.

Question 02 • Easy [4 Marks]

What is a Computer Network?

Official Model Answer & Marking Scheme [4 Marks]:

A Computer Network is an interconnected collection of autonomous computing devices capable of routing traffic, sharing hardware resources, and delivering services.

Question 03 • Easy [5 Marks]

Compare Data Communications vs Computer Networks.

Official Model Answer & Marking Scheme [5 Marks]:

Data Communications provides theoretical rules, signals, and protocol mechanics; Computer Networks is the practical hardware deployment and real-world implementation.

Question 04 • Easy [5 Marks]

List the 5 essential components of Data Communication.

Official Model Answer & Marking Scheme [5 Marks]:

1. Message (Data payload) 2. Sender (Origin unit) 3. Receiver (Destination unit) 4. Medium (Guided/Unguided path) 5. Protocol (Governing rules)

Question 05 • Easy [3 Marks]

Define the Message component.

Official Model Answer & Marking Scheme [3 Marks]:

The actual data information being sent (text, audio, video, picture).

Question 06 • Easy [3 Marks]

Define the Sender component.

Official Model Answer & Marking Scheme [3 Marks]:

The originating device hardware capable of transmitting a signal across a medium.

Question 07 • Easy [3 Marks]

Define the Receiver component.

Official Model Answer & Marking Scheme [3 Marks]:

The target device hardware designated to receive and process a signal.

Question 08 • Easy [3 Marks]

Define Transmission Medium.

Official Model Answer & Marking Scheme [3 Marks]:

The physical path traversed by a signal from sender to receiver.

Question 09 • Easy [4 Marks]

Define Protocol.

Official Model Answer & Marking Scheme [4 Marks]:

A set of governing rules synchronizing communication formatting, timing, and sequence.

Question 10 • Easy [4 Marks]

Why is a Protocol mandatory?

Official Model Answer & Marking Scheme [4 Marks]:

Without a protocol, devices can physically send signals but cannot parse or interpret the bit stream.

Question 11 • Easy [4 Marks]

Define Simplex mode with an example.

Official Model Answer & Marking Scheme [4 Marks]:

Unidirectional communication (e.g. FM Radio, Television broadcast, Keyboard to PC).

Question 12 • Easy [4 Marks]

Define Half-Duplex mode with an example.

Official Model Answer & Marking Scheme [4 Marks]:

Bidirectional communication, one direction at a time (e.g. Walkie-Talkies, Legacy Hubs).

Question 13 • Easy [4 Marks]

Define Full-Duplex mode with an example.

Official Model Answer & Marking Scheme [4 Marks]:

Simultaneous bidirectional communication (e.g. Modern Switches, Telephone call).

Question 14 • Easy [3 Marks]

Define Guided Media.

Official Model Answer & Marking Scheme [3 Marks]:

Physical solid conductors (CAT6 UTP, STP, Coaxial, Fiber Optic).

Question 15 • Easy [3 Marks]

Define Unguided Media.

Official Model Answer & Marking Scheme [3 Marks]:

Wireless electromagnetic transmission through air/vacuum (Wi-Fi RF, Microwave, Infrared).

Question 16 • Easy [4 Marks]

Why is liquid the #1 physical enemy of networking?

Official Model Answer & Marking Scheme [4 Marks]:

Water short-circuits copper conductors and strongly absorbs/scatters 2.4GHz/5GHz radio waves.

Question 17 • Easy [4 Marks]

List 4 business reasons for computer networking.

Official Model Answer & Marking Scheme [4 Marks]:

1. File & resource sharing 2. Peripheral sharing 3. Centralized administration 4. Cost reduction

Question 18 • Easy [3 Marks]

Define Analog Signal.

Official Model Answer & Marking Scheme [3 Marks]:

Continuous electromagnetic waveform changing smoothly over time.

Question 19 • Easy [3 Marks]

Define Digital Signal.

Official Model Answer & Marking Scheme [3 Marks]:

Discrete sequence of voltage levels representing binary 1s and 0s.

Question 20 • Easy [3 Marks]

Define Peak Amplitude.

Official Model Answer & Marking Scheme [3 Marks]:

Maximum absolute voltage or signal strength value attained by a signal.

Question 21 • Easy [3 Marks]

Define Signal Frequency (f).

Official Model Answer & Marking Scheme [3 Marks]:

Number of complete cycles per second, measured in Hertz (Hz).

Question 22 • Easy [3 Marks]

Define Signal Period (T).

Official Model Answer & Marking Scheme [3 Marks]:

Time required to complete one full cycle (T = 1 / f).

Question 23 • Easy [3 Marks]

Define Signal Phase.

Official Model Answer & Marking Scheme [3 Marks]:

Position of a wave relative to time t = 0 in degrees or radians.

Question 24 • Easy [3 Marks]

Define Bit Rate.

Official Model Answer & Marking Scheme [3 Marks]:

Number of bits sent per second (bps).

Question 25 • Easy [3 Marks]

Define Baud Rate.

Official Model Answer & Marking Scheme [3 Marks]:

Number of signal symbol state changes per second.

Question 26 • Easy [3 Marks]

When is Bit Rate equal to Baud Rate?

Official Model Answer & Marking Scheme [3 Marks]:

When each symbol carries exactly 1 bit (M = 2).

Question 27 • Easy [3 Marks]

Define Signal Attenuation.

Official Model Answer & Marking Scheme [3 Marks]:

Loss of signal strength/amplitude as a signal propagates through a medium.

Question 28 • Easy [3 Marks]

What unit measures signal loss?

Official Model Answer & Marking Scheme [3 Marks]:

Decibels (dB = 10 * log10(P_out / P_in)).

Question 29 • Easy [3 Marks]

Define Delay Distortion.

Official Model Answer & Marking Scheme [3 Marks]:

Composite signal alteration caused by different frequency components traveling at varying speeds.

Question 30 • Easy [3 Marks]

Define Thermal Noise.

Official Model Answer & Marking Scheme [3 Marks]:

Random electron agitation in conductors creating background noise.

Part 2: Medium / Intermediate Questions (Questions 31 – 40)

Question 31 • Medium [6 Marks]

Calculate Nyquist Bit Rate for 4 kHz noiseless channel with 2 signal levels.

Official Model Answer & Marking Scheme [6 Marks]:

Bit Rate = 2 * B * log2(M) = 2 * 4000 * log2(2) = 8,000 bps (8 Kbps).

Question 32 • Medium [6 Marks]

Calculate Nyquist Bit Rate for 4 kHz channel with 16 signal levels.

Official Model Answer & Marking Scheme [6 Marks]:

Bit Rate = 2 * B * log2(M) = 2 * 4000 * log2(16) = 32,000 bps (32 Kbps).

Question 33 • Medium [6 Marks]

Calculate Shannon Capacity for 1 MHz channel with SNR = 63.

Official Model Answer & Marking Scheme [6 Marks]:

C = B * log2(1 + SNR) = 1,000,000 * log2(64) = 6,000,000 bps (6 Mbps).

Question 34 • Medium [5 Marks]

Convert SNR_dB = 20 dB to unitless SNR.

Official Model Answer & Marking Scheme [5 Marks]:

20 = 10 * log10(SNR) => log10(SNR) = 2 => SNR = 10^2 = 100.

Question 35 • Medium [6 Marks]

Calculate Propagation Delay for 2,000 km optical fiber (s = 2*10^8 m/s).

Official Model Answer & Marking Scheme [6 Marks]:

T_p = distance / speed = 2,000,000 / 2*10^8 = 0.01s = 10 ms.

Question 36 • Medium [6 Marks]

Calculate Transmission Delay for 1,500 byte packet on 100 Mbps link.

Official Model Answer & Marking Scheme [6 Marks]:

Bits = 12,000. T_t = 12,000 / 100,000,000 = 0.12 ms.

Question 37 • Medium [5 Marks]

Calculate signal loss in dB if 100 mW input drops to 10 mW output.

Official Model Answer & Marking Scheme [5 Marks]:

dB = 10 * log10(10 / 100) = 10 * log10(0.1) = -10 dB (10 dB loss).

Question 38 • Medium [6 Marks]

Calculate Baud Rate for 8-PSK scheme delivering 7,200 bps.

Official Model Answer & Marking Scheme [6 Marks]:

Bits per symbol = log2(8) = 3. Baud Rate = 7200 / 3 = 2400 Baud.

Question 39 • Medium [5 Marks]

Explain Manchester Encoding self-clocking mechanics.

Official Model Answer & Marking Scheme [5 Marks]:

Guarantees a voltage transition in the middle of every bit period for clock synchronization.

Question 40 • Medium [5 Marks]

Compare Baseband vs Broadband Transmission.

Official Model Answer & Marking Scheme [5 Marks]:

Baseband sends digital signals directly without modulation. Broadband modulates digital data onto high-frequency carrier waves.

Part 3: Hard / Tricky / Advanced Questions (Questions 41 – 70)

Question 41 • Hard [10 Marks]

Joint Nyquist and Shannon Optimization Problem

Official Model Answer & Marking Scheme [10 Marks]:

1. Shannon Capacity C = 1,000,000 * log2(1+31) = 5 Mbps. 2. Nyquist: 5,000,000 = 2 * 1,000,000 * log2(M) => M = 2^2.5 = 5.65 => Must use M = 6 signal levels.

Question 42 • Hard [8 Marks]

Derive 64-byte Minimum Ethernet Frame Size

Official Model Answer & Marking Scheme [8 Marks]:

Round-trip time 2*T_p across 2,500m cable = 51.2 microseconds slot time. At 10 Mbps, min bits = 10^7 * 51.2*10^-6 = 512 bits = 64 bytes.

Question 43 • Hard [8 Marks]

Stop-and-Wait Protocol Efficiency Calculation

Official Model Answer & Marking Scheme [8 Marks]:

T_t = 1ms, T_p = 5ms. Efficiency = T_t / (T_t + 2*T_p) = 1 / (1 + 10) = 9.09%.

Question 44 • Hard [7 Marks]

Telephone Line Shannon Capacity Math

Official Model Answer & Marking Scheme [7 Marks]:

SNR = 10^(30/10) = 1000. C = 3100 * log2(1001) = 30.9 Kbps.

Question 45 • Hard [8 Marks]

Multi-hop Packet Switching Latency Derivation

Official Model Answer & Marking Scheme [8 Marks]:

Total latency for 3 packets across 4 hops = (Hops + Packets - 1) * T_t = (4 + 3 - 1) * 1ms = 6 ms.

Question 46 • Hard [7 Marks]

PCM Voice Sampling vs Delta Modulation

Official Model Answer & Marking Scheme [7 Marks]:

PCM samples amplitude at Nyquist rate (8kHz) and quantizes to 8-bit PCM (64Kbps). Delta modulation encodes only the +1/-1 change.

Question 47 • Hard [7 Marks]

4B/5B Block Coding Analysis

Official Model Answer & Marking Scheme [7 Marks]:

Maps 4-bit nibbles into 5-bit symbols to eliminate long runs of zeros, ensuring clock synchronization before NRZ-I line encoding.

Question 48 • Hard [6 Marks]

40 Gbps Transceiver Bit Interval Calculation

Official Model Answer & Marking Scheme [6 Marks]:

T_b = 1 / (40 * 10^9) = 25 picoseconds.

Question 49 • Hard [10 Marks]

Infinite Bandwidth Shannon Limit Derivation

Official Model Answer & Marking Scheme [10 Marks]:

As B -> infinity, C = (S / N0) * log2(e) = 1.44 * S / N0.

Question 50 • Hard [8 Marks]

PAM-5 1000BASE-T Line Encoding Math

Official Model Answer & Marking Scheme [8 Marks]:

Uses 5 voltage levels across 4 wire pairs transmitting 2 bits per symbol per pair at 125 Mbaud (125 * 2 * 4 = 1000 Mbps).
