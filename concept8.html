8.1 Quality Fundamentals (Mr Bhuru Nov 10 Lecture)

Quality is NOT Grade

DEFINITIONCORE
Quality
Fit for Use & Meets Reqs
  • Quality: Does the software satisfy user requirements and perform reliably?
  • Grade: Feature-level richness (low grade = basic features; high grade = many features).
  • Low grade + zero bugs = High Quality! High grade + crashes = Low Quality!
WHY IT MATTERSVALUE
  • Maintain Reputation & stakeholder trust.
  • Regulatory Compliance (ISO / IEC standards).
  • Maintainability, Sustainability & Security.
  • Built Over Time: Quality is NOT inspected in at the end—it must be checked throughout!
TRIPLE CONSTRAINTPYRAMID
Tech Debt
When Scope/Time/Cost Tighten
  • Quality sits at the center of Scope, Time & Cost.
  • When squeezed, people blame each other and take shortcuts.
  • Shortcuts create Technical Debt, raising long-term maintenance costs dramatically.
8.2 ISO 9126-1 Quality Model & Software Metrics

Measuring Software: ISO 9126 & Metrics

6 ISO 9126-1 QUALITY CHARACTERISTICS

1. Functionality • 2. Reliability • 3. Usability

Functionality (does required job—prerequisite for all other 5) • Reliability (fault tolerance & recovery) • Usability (UI clarity & learnability).

4. Efficiency • 5. Maintainability • 6. Portability

Efficiency (speed/resource use) • Maintainability (ease of fixes/updates) • Portability (runs across environments).

HOW WE MEASURE QUALITY (BHURU + ISO)

1. Defect Density

Number of confirmed defects per 1,000 lines of code (1 KLOC).

2. Algorithmic Efficiency

Measures runtime responsiveness, latency & resource throughput.

3. Test Coverage & Reviews

Percentage of code/requirements verified by test cases & peer code inspections (Sommerville Ch 24).

8.3 QA vs. QC, Cost of Quality & Change Control

Proactive QA vs. Reactive QC

QUALITY ASSURANCEPROACTIVE
QA
Prevent Defects (Process Focus)
  • Assures standards, processes, and procedures are appropriate and followed.
  • Examples: Process audits, ISO 9001 quality manuals, peer design reviews, coding standards.
QUALITY CONTROLREACTIVE
QC
Detect & Correct Defects (Product)
  • Tests finished or intermediate artifacts to catch bugs before release.
  • Test Pyramid: Many fast Unit Tests (base) → Integration Tests → fewer End-to-End UI Tests (top).
COST & CHANGE CTRLGOVERNANCE
  • Cost of Conformance: Prevention (training, reviews) + Appraisal (testing, audits).
  • Cost of Non-Conformance: Internal rework + external customer failures.
  • Change Management: Never allow undocumented changes—log them as future work unless inevitable!
8.4 Root Cause Analysis — Mr Bhuru Nov 10 Lecture

Ishikawa (Fishbone): Why Projects Fail (6Ms)

TOP BONES (3 BRANCHES)3+ CAUSES EACH
  • 1. Technology: Immature/new tools, legacy integration incompatibility, wrong tech stack choice.
  • 2. Resources: Insufficient funding/budget, weak dev hardware/environments, missing server infra.
  • 3. Measurements: Unrealistic SWAG estimates, missing EVM tracking (SPI/CPI), zero quality/defect metrics.
BOTTOM BONES (3 BRANCHES)3+ CAUSES EACH
  • 4. Environment: Shifting regulations/laws, organizational politics / low exec support, power/network outages.
  • 5. Methods: Scope creep / unclear SRS, skipping Change Control, wrong SDLC or missing WBS.
  • 6. Manpower: Staff turnover/shortfalls, skill gaps/inexperience, poor team communication.

Spine → PROJECT FAILURE | Exam Instruction from Mr Bhuru

"Keep fish simple: Technology, Resources, Measurements (top) • Environment, Methods, Manpower (bottom) — HAVE AT LEAST 3 CAUSES FOR EACH!"
